# Remaining critiques and defensible claim boundary

## What is defensible now

The distribution is an inspectable, reproducible computational teaching prototype.
Its volume, tangent-compliance and inverse-pressure operations are mutually
consistent at fixed relay state; oxygen content is conserved by its shunt-mixing
equation. It includes numerical convergence checks, an analytical one-compartment
work check, and paired-language comparisons. These are software properties under
explicit assumptions, not evidence that the assumptions fit human physiology.

## Scientific limitations that code cleanup cannot remove

1. **Phenomenological mechanics.** The exponential stiffening law, pressure scales,
   fractions and relay distributions were assumed, not estimated from independent
   measurements. A compliance maximum generated by these choices is not evidence
   of an optimal or safe clinical PEEP. Lung/chest-wall partition is absent.
2. **History without time.** Relays settle instantly and remain fixed during a
   breath. There is no expiratory RC trajectory, auto-PEEP, opening kinetics,
   intratidal recruitment or breath-by-breath accumulation. A 30 cmH2O conditioning
   step is only a state initializer. Some relays can remain closed at that pressure.
3. **AOP abstraction.** A single AOP subtracts from every compartment's distending
   pressure and shifts relay thresholds. This is not a regional airway-closure
   mechanism. Mechanics is explicitly undefined when set PEEP is below AOP.
4. **Finite-capacity inverse.** Pressure inversion uses a numerical upper bracket
   of AOP+1000 cmH2O and rejects unbracketed targets. Positive parameters are not
   proof of physiological plausibility; extreme configurations are unsupported.
   The 8-4096 relay limit is a software resource guard, not a physiological bound.
5. **Pure-shunt oxygen transport.** One alveolar PO2, fixed SvO2, a prescribed
   dead-space fraction and fixed P50 cannot represent full V/Q heterogeneity,
   metabolic/cardiac-output feedback, dyshemoglobinemia or oximeter artifacts.
   Fixed bicarbonate with changing CO2 is not a whole-body acid-base model.
6. **Endpoint index.** R/I* depends on Vt, chosen histories, AOP, elastic shape and
   tissue fractions. Its exact model recruitment component and nonlinear bias are
   useful diagnostics, not independently measured biological recruitment. No
   numerical or directional agreement with the Chen patient measurements is claimed.
7. **Work convention.** Inspiratory airway work includes the PEEP baseline and
   resistance. It is neither total patient-plus-ventilator work nor dissipated
   parenchymal energy; a numerical value cannot establish injury risk in this model.
8. **Verification dependence.** Python was the reference for the JS port. Shared
   assumptions and shared mistakes can survive translation checks. The original
   reference and this rewrite were AI-assisted; an independent domain review is
   still needed. Analytical checks cover only limited special cases.
9. **Sparse exploration.** Four cases, selected sweeps and a small seeded multifactor
   set are not exhaustive testing, uncertainty quantification or parameter
   identifiability analysis. Tight translation tolerance is not a clinical error bound.
10. **Pedagogy untested.** Inspectability and offline access do not establish learning
    benefits. Learning objectives, usability, misconceptions, retention and transfer
    require user studies. No efficacy or equivalence claim is supported.

## Next evidence, matched to the claim

- Independent numerical review and additional analytical limiting-case solutions.
- Prespecified mechanical bench comparisons for supported passive VCV states,
  reporting bias/error and failures. A mechanical bench cannot validate the gas model.
- Separate gas-exchange reference comparisons across documented Hb, venous boundary,
  FiO2 and shunt domains; independent data if making patient-relevant accuracy claims.
- Expert appraisal focused on potential teaching misconceptions, followed by a
  usability study and an appropriately designed learning-outcome study.

Do not add retuning merely to make four archetypes appear to reproduce a cohort.
Keep the title centered on development and computational verification until new
evidence supports a stronger claim. Publication readiness also requires the
owner decisions and browser checks in PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST.md.
