Snapshot pressure history

An empty history uses one step at the set PEEP. Relays settle instantly at each step; no elapsed time is simulated.

2. Snapshot

SaO2 is modeled arterial saturation, not a pulse-oximeter SpO2 reading. Inspiratory work includes the PEEP baseline and lumped resistive work; it is not tissue-dissipated energy or a treatment threshold.

3. Signed R/I-style endpoint analogue

Independent protocol: initially closed relays, then 30 → 15 → max(5, AOP) cmH2O. This panel does not inherit the snapshot history. It does not simulate an expired breath.

Negative values remain visible. The index combines modeled recruitment with a nonlinear inflation-reference bias; no recruiter categories or clinical cutoff are assigned.

4. Decremental PEEP-compliance sweep

Independent protocol: initially closed relays, condition at 30, then 20 down to 4 cmH2O in steps of 2. This is not a conventional quasi-static P/V loop.

The sampled compliance maximum is not a recommended PEEP, even when it lies inside the sweep. Hemodynamic effects and clinical outcomes are absent.

What to explore

  1. Load Injury C, compute its single-step snapshot, then select the conditioned history. The same set PEEP can have different outputs because relay history differs.
  2. Change Vt while holding history fixed. Compare tidal and tangent compliance; they need not be equal in a nonlinear model.
  3. Load Baseline and examine the signed endpoint index. Nonlinear inflation can produce a nonzero index even with zero recruitable tissue.

These are model-based questions, not tested lessons or ventilation instructions. No spontaneous breathing, expiration, auto-PEEP, intratidal recruitment, regional V/Q distribution, or hemodynamic feedback is simulated.
