## Project use

These documents define the broader strategic direction for the HumMod/vent work. The immediate software remains a research prototype. Development should prioritize reproducibility, provenance, HumMod fidelity, independent physiologic validation, and cardiopulmonary coupling before broader commercialization claims.

The intended first commercial product is an **in-silico cardiopulmonary R&D and testing platform**, not bedside treatment recommendation software. Patient-specific clinical decision support is a later-stage objective that depends on prospective validation and an appropriate regulatory pathway.
